How the difficulty and metrics work

Each completed target records its delay from appearance until a successful click, target diameter, pointer position when the target appeared, and sampled movement path. Missed clicks and timed-out targets lower the five-trial adaptive accuracy. For mouse/pen users, the Fitts-style estimate uses pointer-to-target distance measured at target appearance (not the previous target location): sum of log2(distance/diameter + 1) divided by the sum of acquisition times. It mixes reaction and movement time, is not a standardized Fitts test, and is disabled for touch input. Old JSON exports used a different distance method, so their throughput is not directly compared. This is a prototype for WarriorHacks 2.0.
